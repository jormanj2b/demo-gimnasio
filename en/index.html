What happens in your trial week

  1. 01

    15-minute assessment

    We check your mobility and basic strength and talk through any injuries. Your coach uses that to adjust every exercise.

  2. 02

    Up to 5 classes

    Drop into any class you like. Each one has a warm-up, a strength block and a short, hard finisher.

  3. 03

    Decide, no pressure

    On Friday we walk you through the plans. If it’s not for you, that’s fine. We never asked for your card.

Small groups, all ages
